### 🚀 About me

After 8 years in tech support, I made the jump into something I'd always been drawn to: building software. I recently completed the **Swift Developer Program** at Apple Coding Academy, and now I build **100% native iOS apps** with Swift and SwiftUI — no templates, no shortcuts.
